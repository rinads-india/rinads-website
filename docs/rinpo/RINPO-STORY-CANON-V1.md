# RINPO Story Canon v1.0
## Fact, Founder History, Product Truth and RINADS Universe Lore

**Status:** canonical narrative framework  
**Rule:** historical fact, product capability, roadmap and lore must never be silently mixed.

## 1. Four narrative layers

### A. Founder History — factual / evidence-led
The founder story belongs to **Andrino T D**, not to RINPO.

```text
ANDRINO
Curiosity
  ↓
Family technology / photography / computers / internet / games
  ↓
MOONKNIGHT
Creative identity, photography, editing, visual work
  ↓
Digital marketing + client operations + business complexity
  ↓
RINADS
Systems, software, operating architecture
  ↓
RINADS INTELLIGENCE
  ↓
RINPO RUNTIME
  ↓
RINPO
Humanized Digital Intelligence
  ↓
RINADS WORLD
Spatial / interactive experience
```

### B. Product Truth — technical
RINPO is the humanized interface to the RINADS Intelligence + Execution Runtime.

RINPO does not literally become conscious, override policy, or replace the authoritative business database.

### C. Roadmap — future
Private inference, broader autonomy, RINADS World, larger industry packs and expanded local-first infrastructure remain roadmap items unless actually deployed and verified.

### D. RINADS Universe Lore — symbolic
The banyan-tree awakening, child-genius origin and “becoming AI” transformation may remain as mythology inside films, RINADS World, campaigns or story mode.

Lore must not be presented as historical biography or technical documentation.
